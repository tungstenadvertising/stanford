Stanford

A campus like a town. A location like a city.

At Stanford, education unfolds at the intersection of academic excellence, transformative research, and an environment unlike any other.

8,000 acres of possibility - in the heart of Silicon Valley.

Stanford offers a unique hybrid experience: the immersive focus of a self-contained academic community - with direct access to one of the world's most dynamic regions for innovation, healthcare, and biotechnology. It is not just a college town, nor is it a traditional urban campus. It is the best of both worlds, serene, resource-rich, and globally connected.

A Campus with Reach

Stanford's unique location positions students at the epicenter of groundbreaking medical and technological advancement. From your first day at Stanford, you are part of an ecosystem shaped by world-renowned hospitals, biomedical research institutes, and pioneering biotech companies.

Five minutes from campus, you'll find startups redefining health technology. Steps away, trails wind through the Stanford Dish. You will be part of environment that fosters both intellectual pursuit and essential balance.

While academic rigor defines the Stanford experience, life here extends well beyond the classroom or clinic. The campus provides a remarkable quality of life - marked by open spaces, cultural vibrancy, and a deep sense of community. Nearby cities like Palo Alto and San Francisco offer further access to arts, diversity, and professional networks. You will study in a place where ideas are not only cultivated, but translated into action. Where your education is grounded in purpose, and your environment accelerates what's possible.

Land Acknowledgement

Stanford University sits on the ancestral land of the Muwekma Ohlone Tribe. This land was and continues to be of great importance of the Ohlone people. Consistent with our values of community and inclusion, we have a responsibility to acknowledge, honor, and make visible the University's relationship to Native peoples.
